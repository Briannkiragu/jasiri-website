JASIRI

Jasiri is a wakeup call to brave souls ready to take risks and do things without fear of judgement or what society may think. In a world where everyone is watching everyone, Jasiri reminds rebels to look within and make peace with themselves before seeking external validation.

We believe what you wear can be an expression of who you are: honest, personal, and unapologetically your own.

FREEDOM ARC

Our community takes inspiration from the Mau Mau freedom fighters, who were ready to risk their lives for the freedom of the people. The core principle of the brand is to set the soul free—to wander, do what it truly wants, and express that through art and fashion.

MCMLXIII (1963) represents the year Kenya gained independence. As a brand rooted in Nairobi, it reminds us that freedom is achievable through conscious effort and self-confidence.
